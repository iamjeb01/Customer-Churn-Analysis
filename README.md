## Project Title
### Customer Churn Analysis for a Bank

---

## Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing customer churn in a bank using an interactive dashboard. The goal is to identify patterns and factors contributing to customer attrition and provide actionable insights that can help improve customer retention. The analysis examines churn across demographics, tenure, credit score, account balance, and branch locations.

---


## Business Problem & Objectives
### Business Problem
Customer churn is a major challenge for banks, as acquiring new customers is often more expensive than retaining existing ones. The bank lacks clear visibility into why customers are leaving and which customer segments are most at risk, making it difficult to design effective retention strategies.

### Objectives
The objectives of this analysis are to:

- Measure the overall customer churn rate

- Identify customer segments with the highest churn risk

- Analyze how factors such as tenure, age, credit score, balance, gender, and branch location influence churn

- Provide data-driven insights to support customer retention and business decision-making.

---

## Data Source
The dataset represents 10,000 bank customers and includes customer demographic information, account details, credit scores, tenure, balance ranges, and churn status. The data was provided in a structured format suitable for analysis and visualization.

## Tools Used

## Data Cleaning & Preparation
### The following data preparation steps were carried out:

- Removed duplicate records

- Handled missing and inconsistent values

- Standardized categorical fields (age ranges, balance ranges, tenure groups)

- Created calculated columns for churn status and customer segmentation

- Validated data types and relationships between tables

## Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)
EDA was conducted to understand the structure and distribution of the data. Key activities included:

- Analyzing customer distribution by branch, gender, and age range

- Examining churn patterns across tenure groups

- Reviewing churn rates by credit score and balance ranges

- Identifying outliers and unusual trends

---

## Data Analysis
The analysis focused on answering the following business questions:

- Which customer segments have the highest churn rate?

- How does tenure affect customer retention?

- What is the relationship between credit score, balance, and churn?

- Are there branch-level differences in customer churn?

- Interactive visuals were used to compare churn rates across multiple dimensions.

## Key Findings

1. The overall churn rate is 20.4%

2. Customers with less than 1 year tenure show the highest churn

3. Higher churn is observed among customers with lower credit scores

4. Customers with lower account balances are more likely to exit

5. Churn varies across branches, indicating regional performance differences

6. Middle-aged customers show notable churn compared to younger groups

---

## Recommendations
1. Improve onboarding and engagement for new customers within the first year

2. Introduce targeted retention strategies for low-balance and low-credit-score customers

3. Develop branch-specific action plans based on churn performance

4. Use predictive churn indicators to proactively retain at-risk customers

5. Continuously monitor churn metrics using dashboards for data-driven decisions
